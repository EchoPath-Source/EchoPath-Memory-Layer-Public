# Preset Pack Starter Edition — Public Summary

This folder contains a public-safe summary of the EchoPath Memory Layer Preset Pack Starter Edition.

The summary names ten starter behavior templates and describes their design intent without exposing private runtime internals, private tuning constants, protected research details, or future-stack implementation details.

## Included Presets

| ID | Name | Category | One-line behavior summary |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `haunted_room` | Haunted Room | atmosphere | A room becomes more unsettling as suspicious or fearful activity repeats. |
| `predator_trail` | Predator Trail | navigation-pressure | Repeated movement leaves a trail that can attract or guide a hunter-like response. |
| `safe_path` | Safe Path | guidance | Frequently successful routes become recognizable as safer paths. |
| `danger_echo` | Danger Echo | hazard-memory | Dangerous locations retain warning pressure after repeated threats. |
| `loot_memory` | Loot Memory | exploration | Looted or valuable areas remember player attention and can change future incentives. |
| `crowd_wear` | Crowd Wear | social-space | Repeated crowd movement makes a space feel worn, busy, or socially stressed. |
| `shrine_activation` | Shrine Activation | ritual-interaction | Repeated interactions build toward a visible ritual or shrine response. |
| `animal_territory` | Animal Territory | territory | Creature presence and player intrusion shape territorial pressure. |
| `npc_habit` | NPC Habit | routine | Repeated NPC behavior makes locations feel habitual and expected. |
| `encounter_scar` | Encounter Scar | aftermath | Intense encounters leave a persistent scar that can affect later tone or behavior. |
